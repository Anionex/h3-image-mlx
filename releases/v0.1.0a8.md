H3 now uses wider INT8 TensorOps tiles, page-aligned weight reads and overlapped layer loading for resident inference on M5. The original six-step sampler and weights are preserved.

On an M5 Pro with 48 GB, median 1024×1024 generation time went from 26.49 s in a7 to 18.93 s in a8 (28.5% lower latency). Median peak process footprint went from 21.08 GB to 21.67 GB. All six interleaved runs produced byte-identical PNGs with uncached weight reads.
