H3 now selects a tiled Metal SIMD kernel for eligible large INT8 projections and uses aligned direct weight reads on the legacy Apple Silicon GPU path.

On a CircleCI M4 Pro VM running macOS 26.3, median 1024×1024 generation time with streamed weights fell from 250.55 s to 101.56 s (59.5% lower latency). Median peak stage-process footprint was 2.44 GB in a8 and 2.30 GB in a9. Six interleaved renders produced identical PNG files.
